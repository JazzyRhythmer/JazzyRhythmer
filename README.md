# Jazzy Rhythmer

![Two sides of the FM-1. Discover a sound with JAZZY. Build a groove with RHYTHMER.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JazzyRhythmer/Rhythmer/main/docs/github/firmware-lineup.png)

I make music and instruments for finding the next groove. **JAZZY** and **RHYTHMER** are two free, open-source firmwares for the M-VAVE FM-1.

| JAZZY | RHYTHMER |
| --- | --- |
| [![JAZZY — Explore the unheard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JazzyRhythmer/Jazzy/main/docs/github/hero.png)](https://github.com/JazzyRhythmer/Jazzy) | [![RHYTHMER — Sound into motion](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JazzyRhythmer/Rhythmer/main/docs/github/hero.png)](https://github.com/JazzyRhythmer/Rhythmer) |
| **Explore the unheard.** Pick one sound per track. Browse 31 synth, drum and Break engines and over 1,200 factory sounds. | **Sound into motion.** Build and perform grooves with assignable tracks, individual drum swaps, per-track generation and break chops. |
